What to write to us about

Getting the Calling Agent for your business. The most useful first message includes: your type of business and city, the number your customers call today, roughly how many calls you receive (and miss) in a day, and what a typical call asks for — bookings, appointments, prices, hours. With those facts we can say honestly whether the agent fits your phones and what installation on your own number would involve.

Questions about the demo. The demo call is a simulation, and we are glad to hear what felt right and what did not — a question the agent should have asked, a detail it should have captured. Tell us which scenario you played and what you noticed.

Scenarios and languages. If your trade is not among the demo businesses, or your callers mostly speak a mix of Urdu and English, tell us — the scenario list and the agent's scripts grow from what business owners ask for.

What happens next

For installation enquiries: a reply within 1–2 working days, a walkthrough of the demo together on a scenario like your own calls, and then a setup plan — your number, your services and prices, your booking rules, your sheet. Call details are written to your own bookings sheet in your own account.

Please do not send

Never email us passwords, one-time codes or payment details, and please do not send recordings or lists containing your customers' personal information — a description of the kind of calls you receive is always enough.
