About Loona Calling Agent

Loona Calling Agent · Loona Automation

Loona Calling Agent is an AI voice agent that answers a business's phone calls — every call, every hour. It picks up in under a second, talks to the caller like a trained receptionist, takes the booking or appointment, saves every detail to a bookings sheet, and alerts the owner the moment it happens. No missed calls, no lost customers, and no "sorry, we were closed."

What it does on a call

The agent answers in English or Urdu, works out what the caller needs — a booking, an appointment, a price, opening hours, directions — and asks the natural follow-up questions a receptionist would ask: which day, what time, how many people, a name and number for the booking. It then confirms the booking back to the caller, writes the details into the business's bookings sheet as a clean, searchable record the whole team can open, and sends the owner an instant alert. A call at 2 p.m. and a call at 2 a.m. are handled exactly the same way.

How it works — honestly

The demo on this page is a simulation that runs in your browser: pick a business, press play, and watch a call from start to finish — answer, conversation, booking, sheet entry, owner alert. It is a faithful picture of the job, not a live phone line. The real system is installed for each business individually, on the business's own number, where it connects live telephony with AI speech and understanding, and writes to the business's own bookings sheet. Loona Automation sets the system up around the business's services, prices, hours and booking rules, so the agent only ever says what the business has approved.

Who it is for

Hotels, clinics, salons, restaurants, workshops and service businesses — anywhere the phone ringing unanswered means a customer who books somewhere else. It matters most after hours and at peak hours: the two moments when a small team physically cannot reach the phone, and the two moments when callers are least willing to try again later.

What it cannot do

The Calling Agent handles routine calls — bookings, appointments, hours, prices, services, directions. It does not improvise prices or policies it was not given, and it does not try to settle a complaint or an unusual request by guessing: it takes the caller's details and flags the call to the owner, so a human can call back with the full picture in front of them. Every call is logged either way, so nothing a caller said is ever left to memory or a scrap of paper.

Why this is free

Loona Automation's founding belief is that the door in front of a tool matters as much as the tool: accounts, paywalls and trials that expire mid-job are where most people give up. The simulated demo on this page is free to play as often as you like, with no sign-up — it is the quickest way to hear what the agent would do with your own callers. The site is supported by Loona Automation and may show advertising, but using it costs nothing.

Who builds it

Loona Calling Agent is part of Loona Automation, founded by Aurang Zeb Loona — a school teacher and AI teacher trainer in Jaranwala, Faisalabad, Punjab, Pakistan — as one part of a network of 918 free tools published in 40 languages.
