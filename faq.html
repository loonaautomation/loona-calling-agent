Loona Calling Agent — Frequently Asked Questions

Loona Calling Agent · Loona Automation

Is the Loona Calling Agent demo free?

Yes. Pick a business scenario and press play — the simulated call runs in your browser, free, with no sign-up. You will see the whole job: the answer, the conversation, the booking, the sheet entry and the owner alert.

Is the demo a real phone call?

No — it is a simulation, and the page says so. It shows faithfully what the agent does on a call, but no real phone line is involved. The live system is installed for each business individually and runs on that business's own number.

Which languages does the Calling Agent speak?

English and Urdu. It answers in the caller's language and can handle the natural mix of the two that real callers use, the way a good local receptionist does.

What kinds of calls can it handle?

Routine business calls: bookings and appointments, opening hours, prices, services, availability and directions. It asks the follow-up questions a receptionist would ask — day, time, party size, name and contact number — and confirms the booking before saving it.

What happens after a call ends?

Every detail the agent collected is written to the business's bookings sheet — a clean, searchable record the whole team can open — and the owner gets an instant alert. Nothing is left to memory or written on a scrap of paper.

What if a caller asks something unusual or complains?

The agent does not guess. It takes the caller's details and flags the call to the owner, so a human can call back with the full picture. Complaints and special requests are deliberately routed to a person, not handled by improvisation.

Does it really answer at night and on holidays?

Yes — that is the point of it. The agent answers 24/7 with pickup in under a second and no ringing out, so the calls a small team physically cannot reach — after closing, during the rush, on a holiday — are still answered and booked.

Email loonaautomation@gmail.com with your business type, city, the number customers call today and roughly how many calls you receive and miss — or book through Fiverr. We reply within 1–2 working days with an honest view of fit and what setup would involve.
